What we
look for

A claim, not a topic. Something most people currently believe that you think is wrong, and can show is wrong.

The idea

Contrarian and specific. If a reader already agrees with the first line, there is no book in it. We want the argument that gets you disagreed with at dinner, then turns out to be right.

It does not have to be finished. Most of what reaches us is a nugget rather than a scoped book, and that is the stage we prefer to see it.

The author

Someone who wants to be argued with. We break and build the idea with you. We push on the weak joint, ask where the evidence actually came from, and keep going until the argument holds or it does not.

What gets published is rarely what arrived. If that reads as interference, we are the wrong press. If it reads as the reason to write to us at all, write.

The subjects

Geopolitics, technology, society and spirituality. We care most about where they cross: the theology inside a trading system, the foreign policy inside a chip, the social order inside a model's training data.

Anyone can write about one of these. We are looking for writers who can hold two at once.

How to start

Send the idea while it is still small. A page or two, one claim, in your own words. Not a manuscript, not a proposal, no comparable titles.

We publish books. Only books, and few of them.
